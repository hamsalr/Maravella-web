Candle Making

Follow the steps while learning

Steps To Make A Candle:

  1. Prepare materials
    • Wax
    • Wick
    • Fragrance oil
    • Container
  2. Melt the wax
    • Use low heat
    • Stir gently
  3. Add fragrance oil
    • Add a few drops
  4. Pour into the container
    • Place the wick in the center
  5. Let it cool
    • Leave it until it hardens
