Candle Making

Introduction

Candle making is a creative and enjoyable craft that allows learners to design scented and decorative candles at home. In this lesson, students will explore the main materials and tools needed to create a simple candle safely and neatly.

Materials & Tools

Material Quantity Purpose
Wax 500g Main ingredient for making the candle
Wick 1 Piece Used to light the candle
Fragrance Oil Few Drops Adds scent to the candle
Container 1 Holds the melted wax
