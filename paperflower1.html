Paper Flower Making

Introduction

Paper flower making is a simple and creative handicraft that allows learners to turn colored paper into beautiful decorative flowers. In this lesson, students will explore the basic materials and tools used to create paper flowers and understand how each item is used in the process.

Materials & Tools

Material Quantity Purpose
Colored Paper 3 Sheets Main material for creating the flower petals
Scissors 1 Pair Used to cut paper into shapes
Glue 1 Stick Helps attach petals together
Wooden Stick 1 Piece Used as the flower stem
