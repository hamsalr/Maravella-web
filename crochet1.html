Crochet Basics

Introduction

Crochet is a relaxing handicraft that uses yarn and a hook to create handmade items such as scarves, flowers, and decorative pieces. In this lesson, learners will get to know the essential materials and tools needed to begin crocheting confidently.

Materials & Tools

Material Quantity Purpose
Yarn 1 Ball Main material for crochet work
Crochet Hook 1 Piece Used to loop and pull the yarn
Scissors 1 Pair To cut the yarn
Stitch Marker 2 Pieces To track stitches and rows
