Introduction To Pottery

Follow the steps while learning

Steps To Make A Pottery Piece:

  1. Prepare materials
    • Clay
    • Water
    • Pottery tools
    • Sponge
  2. Knead the clay well
    • Press and fold the clay
    • Remove air bubbles
  3. Shape the clay into a simple bowl or vase
    • Use your hands to form the base
    • Adjust the edges carefully
  4. Smooth the surface using water and sponge
    • Dip the sponge lightly in water
    • Gently clean rough areas
  5. Add simple details using pottery tools
    • Carve lines or patterns
    • Refine the final shape
  6. Leave it to dry
    • Place it in a safe dry area
    • Wait until it becomes firm
